# PaperTrail grounding policy

When making claims using this repository's research index:

1. Use a named PaperTrail snapshot when reproducibility matters.
2. Retrieve exact evidence passages before making substantive claims.
3. Preserve paper IDs and evidence IDs in research notes.
4. Treat preprints, peer-reviewed papers, reviews, and system synthesis as different source classes.
5. Check for counterevidence; an empty result is not evidence of absence.
6. Do not claim novelty beyond the indexed corpus.
7. Do not fabricate citations when retrieval is empty.
8. Save generated research artifacts under `research/` with an evidence manifest.
9. Report snapshot coverage, gaps, and warnings.
